综合练习

写作 · Compare Schools in China and the U.S.

Your task
  1. Write a comparison in Chinese using the schools, readings, and experiences we discussed in class.
  2. Choose exactly three of the five aspects below. Compare the same aspect on both sides in each body paragraph. Use specific schools or examples; one school does not represent a whole country.
  3. Write five paragraphs: an introduction, three body paragraphs, and a conclusion. In the introduction, name what you are comparing and your three aspects.
  4. For each body paragraph, explain a similarity or difference, give at least one specific example from our readings or discussion, and explain what it may mean for students or families.
  5. Use expressions from this lesson where they fit, such as 首先、其次、最后、而……（则）……、因此、总的来说、对……来说 and 有所不同.
  6. End with your own view and explain why. Preview your essay, copy it into your homework Google Doc, and submit it through Google Classroom.
